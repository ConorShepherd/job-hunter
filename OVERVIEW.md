# job-hunter

A personal tool that turns a CV into a ranked shortlist of real job openings — no manual searching required.

**What it does:**
- Give it your CV (a PDF).
- It works out what job titles you should actually be searching for — including some you might not have thought of yourself.
- It searches multiple job boards for you at once.
- It strips out duplicates and anything from companies you've said you're not interested in.
- It scores every result on how good a fit it actually is, with a one-line reason for each score.
- You get one clean, browsable results page — sortable, searchable, no scrolling through walls of text.

**What makes it "yours":** set a few preferences once — how far you'll commute, whether remote/relocation is on the table, a salary floor, companies to avoid — and every future search respects them automatically.

**Why bother:** it skips the tedious part (deciding what to search for and where), surfaces roles you wouldn't have thought to look for, and doesn't waste your time re-showing jobs you've already seen. One command in, one report out.
